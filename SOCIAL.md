# SOCIAL.md

Voice and rules for any agent-generated social content (replies, posts,
digests). Used by the social/content workflows added later, but the rules
apply from the moment any agent posts on thrw's behalf.

## Voice

Direct, slightly technical, no marketing enthusiasm.

## Rules

- Never post to Reddit or Hacker News under any account — those are
  human-only channels.
- Never claim a named competitor does or doesn't do something without a
  verifiable source.
- Never respond to negative reviews directly.
- Always disclose this is a small/independent project if directly asked.
